# Requirements & Specifications

This section contains product requirements documents (PRDs) and functional specifications.

## Documents

### [Marketing Website PRD](marketing-website-prd.md) ⭐ **PRIMARY**
**Purpose**: Requirements for THIS project - the Moon Ring marketing and conversion website

**Scope**: Homepage, blog, pricing, demo, contact form, email capture, Stripe integration

**When to read**: Before starting any development work

### [Platform PRD](platform-prd.md) 📚 **REFERENCE ONLY**
**Purpose**: Requirements for the full Moon Ring platform (mobile apps, wearable integrations, social features)

**Status**: Reference material explaining the product we're MARKETING (not building in this repo)

**When to read**: To understand the product being sold, for accurate marketing content

### [Conversion Tracking](conversion-tracking.md)
**Purpose**: Analytics and conversion tracking architecture

**Scope**: Vercel Analytics, custom conversion events, attribution

**When to read**: When implementing analytics or conversion funnels

## Important Distinctions

### What We're Building (This Repo)
✅ Marketing website (moonring.com)
✅ Blog and content marketing
✅ Email waitlist capture
✅ Stripe checkout integration
✅ Product demonstration page

### What We're NOT Building (Reference Only)
❌ Mobile apps
❌ Wearable device integrations
❌ Social accountability features
❌ User dashboard / platform

**These are described in the Platform PRD for marketing purposes only.**
