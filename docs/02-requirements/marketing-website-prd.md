# Moon Ring Website Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Marketing, Conversion & User Acquisition Focus

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** September 18, 2025
**Project:** Moon Ring Marketing Website Development
**Document Owner:** Digital Marketing & Product Team

---

## Executive Summary

This PRD defines the requirements for the Moon Ring marketing website, designed to drive user acquisition and conversion for the social accountability platform. The website targets 28M frustrated wearable users seeking behavioral change through evidence-based psychology and social accountability.

**Primary Goals:**
- Generate qualified leads and trial signups for Moon Ring platform
- Establish Moon Ring as the definitive behavioral change solution for wearables
- Convert website visitors to paying subscribers through optimized conversion funnels
- Support both individual consumer and corporate wellness market segments

## 1. STRATEGIC POSITIONING & MESSAGING

### 1.1 Core Value Proposition Hierarchy

**Primary Value Proposition:**
"Transform your abandoned wearable into lasting behavioral change through social accountability"

**Target Segment Value Props:**

#### Frustrated Wearable Users (28M market)
- **Headline:** "Finally unlock the potential of your Apple Watch/Fitbit"
- **Sub-message:** "Turn collected data into real behavioral change with social accountability"
- **Pain Point:** "Tired of tracking without changing?"

#### Accountability Seekers (15M subset)
- **Headline:** "Get the accountability you need to stick to health goals"
- **Sub-message:** "Matched partners and evidence-based psychology for lasting change"
- **Pain Point:** "Ready to commit but need support?"

#### Corporate Wellness (500K employees)
- **Headline:** "Drive measurable employee health outcomes with existing wearables"
- **Sub-message:** "Social accountability delivers 65%+ retention vs 32% industry average"

## 5. USER EXPERIENCE DESIGN

### 5.1 Design System & Brand Consistency

#### Moon Ring Brand Identity
Following the established Moon Ring Design System to ensure coherent brand experience across website and app.

**Primary Brand Colors:**
- **Pink**: `#FF33BA` - Primary CTAs, key decision points
- **Orange**: `#FF9966` - Secondary brand accent
- **Primary Gradient**: `#FF33BA` → `#FF9966` - Main conversion buttons

**Health Category Colors:**
- **Movement/Steps**: `#F7941D` → `#FFF200` gradient
- **Stress Management**: `#FF5A5A` → `#660000` gradient
- **Sleep**: `#52ACFF` → `#725CFA` gradient
- **Recovery**: `#2CE6FF` → `#006699` gradient

**Background System:**
- **Dark Theme Base**: Deep purple gradient `#1B023A` to `#2D1B69`
- **Card Backgrounds**: `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` with glass-morphism
- **Light Theme Option**: For website readability and accessibility

**Typography Hierarchy (Web Adaptation):**
- **H1 Headlines**: 48-56px bold (responsive)
- **H2 Section Headers**: 32-40px medium
- **H3 Subsections**: 24-28px medium
- **Body Text**: 16-18px regular
- **Metric Values**: 32px bold for key numbers
- **Captions**: 14px regular with reduced opacity

**Component Standards:**
- **Border Radius**: 12px consistently for cards and buttons
- **Button Heights**: 48px minimum for mobile touch targets
- **Card Padding**: 24px standard (increased for web)
- **Max Width**: 1280px container for content
- **Grid System**: 12-column responsive grid

### 5.2 Information Architecture

#### Primary Navigation Structure

Home

│   ├── Blog

#### Mobile Navigation Priority
1. Sign Up / Trial CTA

### 5.3 Visual Design Implementation

#### Hero Section Design
**Background:** Deep purple gradient (`#1B023A` to `#2D1B69`) matching app aesthetic
**Primary CTA:** Pink-orange gradient button (`#FF33BA` → `#FF9966`)
**Glass-morphism Cards:** Feature previews with `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` backgrounds

#### Health Category Sections
Each section uses its respective category colors to maintain consistency:
- **Movement Features:** Orange-yellow gradients for step tracking demos
- **Sleep Features:** Blue-purple gradients for sleep optimization content
- **Stress Features:** Red gradients for stress management showcases
- **Recovery Features:** Cyan-blue gradients for recovery tracking displays

#### Interactive Elements
**Buttons:**
- Primary CTAs: Brand gradient (`#FF33BA` → `#FF9966`)
- Secondary CTAs: Solid category colors or white outlines
- Hover States: 10% brightness increase with smooth transition

**Form Elements:**
- Input Fields: Dark background with subtle glass-morphism
- Focus States: Brand pink glow effect
- Validation: Category colors for success/error states

#### Content Cards
- Glass-morphism styling consistent with app
- 12px border radius throughout
- Subtle backdrop blur for depth
- Category-specific accent colors for icons and highlights

### 5.4 Mobile-First Responsive Design

#### Breakpoint Strategy
- Mobile: 320px - 768px (priority design)
- Tablet: 769px - 1024px
- Desktop: 1025px+ (enhanced experience)

#### Mobile Conversion Optimization
- Thumb-friendly CTA buttons (44px minimum)
- Simplified forms with smart defaults
- Fast-loading images and animations
- Progressive web app capabilities

### 5.5 Accessibility & Performance

#### Accessibility Standards (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Color contrast ratios (4.5:1 minimum)
- Screen reader compatibility
- Keyboard navigation support
- Alt text for all images
- Semantic HTML structure

#### Performance Targets
- **Page Load Speed:** <2 seconds on 3G
- **Core Web Vitals:**
  - LCP: <2.5 seconds
  - FID: <100ms
  - CLS: <0.1
- **Lighthouse Score:** 90+ across all metrics
